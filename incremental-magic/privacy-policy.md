## English

### What we collect

Incremental Magic collects a small amount of **anonymous gameplay telemetry** to improve the game:

- **Anonymous identifier** — a randomly generated ID stored on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, Steam ID, or any account.
- **Gameplay events** — aggregated progress data such as session start/end, dungeon enter/clear, boss results, skill and economy summaries, app version, platform (OS), and selected language.
- **Crash and error reports** — exception type, a truncated stack trace, game version, and platform when an error occurs.

We do **not** collect names, email addresses, Steam IDs, nicknames, chat, precise location, or any directly identifying information. The game has no account system and no in-game purchases.

### How we use it

Telemetry is used only to fix bugs, balance difficulty, and improve the game. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

### Third-party services

- **Google Analytics 4** (Google LLC, USA) — receives the anonymous gameplay events above. Data is processed on Google servers, which may be located outside your country. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Discord webhook** (Discord Inc., USA) — receives crash summaries only (exception type, stack excerpt, version, platform) so the developer is alerted to bugs.

Steam itself (Valve Corporation) processes your purchase and platform data under its own [privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/); we do not receive personal data from Steam.

### Opting out

You can disable telemetry at any time in **Options** in the game. When disabled, no gameplay events or crash reports are sent. [옵션 UI 노출 확인 후 문구 확정]

### Retention

Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics settings (14 months) and then deleted or aggregated. Crash alerts on Discord are periodically purged.

### Your rights

Because the identifier is anonymous, we usually cannot link data to you individually. For any privacy question or deletion request, contact us at the email above. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you may also have rights under GDPR (access, erasure, objection); in California, under the CCPA.

### Children

The game is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if our data practices change.

## 한국어

### 수집하는 정보

Incremental Magic 은 게임 개선을 위해 최소한의 **익명 게임플레이 텔레메트리**를 수집합니다.

- **익명 식별자** — 기기에 저장되는 무작위 생성 ID. 이름·이메일·Steam ID·계정과 연결되지 않습니다.
- **게임플레이 이벤트** — 세션 시작/종료, 던전 입장/클리어, 보스 결과, 스킬·경제 요약 등 집계 데이터와 앱 버전, 플랫폼(OS), 선택 언어.
- **오류/크래시 리포트** — 오류 발생 시 예외 유형, 일부 스택 정보, 게임 버전, 플랫폼.

이름, 이메일, Steam ID, 닉네임, 채팅, 정밀 위치 등 **직접 식별 정보는 수집하지 않습니다.** 게임에는 계정 시스템과 인앱 결제가 없습니다.

버그 수정, 난이도 밸런스, 게임 개선에만 사용합니다. 판매하거나 광고에 사용하지 않습니다.
